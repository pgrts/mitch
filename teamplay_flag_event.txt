teamplay_flag_event
Name:	teamplay_flag_event
Structure:	
short	player	player this event involves
short	carrier	the carrier if needed
short	eventtype	pick up, capture, defend, dropped
byte	home	whether or not the flag was home (only set for TF_FLAGEVENT_PICKEDUP)
byte	team	which team the flag belongs to


Standard Flag Event IDs
1 – Picked Up: A player has successfully touched the flag and is now the carrier.
2 – Captured: The carrier has reached their own team's capture zone with the enemy flag.
3 – Returned: A defender touched their own dropped flag, or it reached its "return timer," sending it back to base.
4 – Dropped: The carrier was killed or manually dropped the flag, leaving it on the ground.
5 – Defended: A specific defensive action occurred, such as killing the enemy carrier near your base or preventing a capture.
