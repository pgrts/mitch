<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Entity_description">Entity description</span></h2>
<p>Detects when the flag (intelligence/australium/bomb) enters a zone. Compare with <a href="/wiki/Func_flag_alert" title="Func flag alert">func_flag_alert</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Inputs">Inputs</span></h2>
<div style="font-style:italic;">Parentname&#58;</div><div style="overflow: auto; background-color: none; border-top:1px solid #444; border-bottom:1px solid #444; border-color: #3A3937; max-height:30em; ;">
<dl><dt><kbd>SetParent</kbd> <kbd>&lt;<a href="/wiki/String" title="String">string</a><span style="display:none"><a href="/wiki/RedirectInput/string" class="mw-redirect" title="RedirectInput/string">RedirectInput/string</a></span>&gt;</kbd></dt>
<dd>Move with this entity. See <a href="/wiki/Entity_Hierarchy_(parenting)" title="Entity Hierarchy (parenting)">Entity Hierarchy (parenting)</a>.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>SetParentAttachment</kbd> <kbd>&lt;<a href="/wiki/String" title="String">string</a><span style="display:none"><a href="/wiki/RedirectInput/string" class="mw-redirect" title="RedirectInput/string">RedirectInput/string</a></span>&gt;</kbd></dt>
<dd>Change this entity to attach to a specific <a href="/wiki/Attachment" class="mw-redirect" title="Attachment">attachment</a> point on its parent. The entity will teleport so that the position of its root <a href="/wiki/Bone" class="mw-redirect" title="Bone">bone</a> matches that of the attachment. Entities must be parented before being sent this input.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>SetParentAttachmentMaintainOffset</kbd> <kbd>&lt;<a href="/wiki/String" title="String">string</a><span style="display:none"><a href="/wiki/RedirectInput/string" class="mw-redirect" title="RedirectInput/string">RedirectInput/string</a></span>&gt;</kbd></dt>
<dd>As above, but without teleporting. The entity retains its position relative to the attachment at the time of the input being received.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>ClearParent</kbd></dt>
<dd>Removes this entity from the the movement hierarchy, leaving it free to move independently.</dd></dl>
</div>
<p><i><span style="border-bottom:1px solid #3A3937;display:block">EnableDisable:</span></i>
</p>
<dl><dt><kbd>Enable / Disable</kbd> <kbd>&lt;<span style="color:grey">void</span>&gt;</kbd></dt>
<dd>Enable/disable this entity from performing its task. It might also disappear from view.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>Test</kbd></dt>
<dd>Send OnStartTouchFlag &amp; OnPickedUpFlag outputs if any player with flag is touching. Send OnEndTouchFlag &amp; OnDroppedFlag outputs if no player with flag is touching.</dd></dl>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Outputs">Outputs</span></h2>
<dl><dt><kbd>OnStartTouchFlag</kbd></dt>
<dd>Sent when a flag or flag carrier first touches the zone.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>OnEndTouchFlag</kbd></dt>
<dd>Sent when a flag or flag carrier stops touching the zone.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>OnDroppedFlag</kbd></dt>
<dd>Sent when a flag is dropped in the zone.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt><kbd>OnPickedUpFlag</kbd></dt>
<dd>Sent when a flag is picked up in the zone.</dd></dl>
