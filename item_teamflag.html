<p><code>item_teamflag</code> is a <a href="/wiki/Model_entity" title="Model entity">model entity</a> <a href="/wiki/Entity_availability_overview" title="Entity availability overview">available</a> in <span style="white-space:nowrap; background:rgb(0,0,0,.16); font-weight:bold; padding:2px; border-radius:4px"><span style="user-select:none; position:relative; bottom:.1em"><a href="/wiki/Team_Fortress_2" title="Team Fortress 2"><img alt="Team Fortress 2" src="/w/images/thumb/8/84/Tf2-16px.png/16px-Tf2-16px.png" decoding="async" width="16" height="16" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/84/Tf2-16px.png/24px-Tf2-16px.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/84/Tf2-16px.png/32px-Tf2-16px.png 2x" data-file-width="256" data-file-height="256" /></a></span> <a href="/wiki/Team_Fortress_2" title="Team Fortress 2"><span title="Team Fortress 2" style="color:#CF7336;">Team Fortress 2</span></a></span>. This is the "flag" in Team Fortress 2, also known as "intelligence". It is also used for the bomb in the Mann vs. Machine gamemode, the australium in Special Delivery, the reactor core in Robot Destruction and the pickup objectives in Player Destruction.
